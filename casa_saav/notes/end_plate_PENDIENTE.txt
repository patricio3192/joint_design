OPEN ITEMS - end_plate_column.m  (2026-10-07)
=============================================

1. Rod material. A193 B7 Fu = 860 / Fy = 720 MPa assumed (ASTM Table 2). Get
   the supplier's mill certificate (Cuenca). Ratios on the rods are low (0.32).

2. Rod holes. The plate is set after casting. Standard 18 mm holes need a
   steel template at casting (2 mm tolerance). DG 1 Table 2.3 recommends much
   larger holes for anchor rods; then plate washers welded on site are needed
   and the shear check changes (DG 1 Sec. 3.5.3: slip, use only 2 rods).
   Friction alone (info): phi mu C = 17.4 kN > Vu = 14.1 kN. Decide.

3. 0.5 hef rule for anchor reinforcement (ACI R17.5.2.1). The script uses the
   real hef = 309 -> 154 mm; bastones are at 56.6 mm. With the edge-reduced
   hef' = 107 mm (17.6.2.1.2) the limit would be 53.3 mm. ACI does not say
   which. Moving the bastones to x = +/-76 gives 52.3 mm (no longer touching
   the VCM corner pair). Engineering judgment.

4. Bastones, straight leg. ld = 1011 mm with "other cases" (Table 25.4.2.3,
   psi_t = 1.3) because each baston touches a VCM bar. If clear spacing >= db,
   cover >= db and stirrups >= minimum hold, ld = 675 mm (flag ld_good). Check
   that the VCM has the length past the column back face (883 or 545 mm).
   Bastones carry 0.90 of their capacity: no margin for a larger Mu.

5. Head plates. Square plates are outside ACI headed-anchor testing (agreed).
   Bending checked as a DG 1-type cantilever from the washer (method to
   confirm). One plate for both rods of a row is also acceptable if checked.

6. Grout. Non-shrink, >= 2 f'c (DG 1 Sec. 3.1): >= 42 MPa. Bearing uses the
   concrete f'c.

7. Steel column above: 4 phi12 anchors (uplift possible). Embedment depth and
   uplift force unknown; not designed here. Only their plan position (x = +/-100,
   z = 100 / 300) is in the clash table. The column axial load is not counted.

8. IPE 240 cantilever flexure / LTB: from the frame model (not in the script).

9. Other load combinations (gravity, reversal) to check at the end. The script
   assumes top in tension and no reversal.

10. Joint shear taken as the beam top-bar tension alone (cantilever tension and
    column shear not subtracted): conservative, ratio 0.18. VCM, VCS and the
    column themselves are not designed here.

11. f'c = 210 kg/cm2 used; 240 is possible (more margin on bearing and ld).

12. Plate thickness: 12 mm governs on the compression side (DG 1, 0.93) with
    ext = 20. A 10 mm plate works with ext <= about 15 (t req 9.89 from the n
    strip, ratio 0.99): possible if 15 is enough for the 6 mm fillet.
