END PLATE, ANCHORS AND BASTONES - IPE 240 CANTILEVER ON CONCRETE COLUMN 400x400
================================================================================
Source: end_plate_column.m (inputs), results in reports/end_plate_column_output.txt. Dimensions in mm.
Coordinates: x along the column face (0 = cantilever axis = column axis),
y down from the top of the beams, z into the column from the concrete face.

SEQUENCE
  1. Column cast up to the beam soffit (y = 350).
  2. Rods (with head plates), bastones and joint hoops placed; rods held by a
     steel template drilled like the end plate (standard 18 holes, PENDIENTE 2).
  3. Joint + beams + column top (105 above the beams) cast in one pour.
  4. Leveling nuts + washers on the rods (inside the 30 grout gap), end plate
     (already welded to the IPE) set, outer washer + nut hand tightened.
  5. Non-shrink grout, 30 thick, between plate and column face (>= 2 f'c).

PLATE
  Material ......... ASTM A36, t = 12  (10 only with ext <= 15, PENDIENTE 12)
  Size ............. 140 wide x 260 high
  Position ......... top edge flush with the top of the IPE and of the beams;
                     IPE web on the plate centreline; plate extends 20 below
                     the IPE bottom flange. 30 grout between plate and column.

HOLES (4 x dia. 18, standard for M16, AISC Table J3.3M)
  Horizontal ....... gage 80: x = -40 and +40 (edge distance 30)
  Tension row ...... 42 below the plate top (32.2 below the top-flange inner face)
  Shear row ........ 198 below the plate top (32.2 above the bottom-flange inner
                     face; 62 above the plate bottom edge)

WELDS (E70XX)
  Top flange ....... CJP groove weld, flange beveled (AWS D1.1 prequalified
                     joint chosen by the fabricator). Check 0.67.
  Bottom flange .... 6 fillet both faces: outer 120, inner 2 x 41.9. Check 0.63.
  Web .............. 6 fillet both sides, full straight web height 190.4.

ANCHOR RODS (4)
  Material ......... ASTM A193 B7, dia. 16, fully threaded (mill certificate,
                     PENDIENTE 1). Same rods for tension and shear rows.
  Embedment ........ head-plate bearing face at z = 309 (hef = 309), inside the
                     hoop cage; rod tip at z = 345 (5 clear to the back hoop).
  Length ........... about 411 (345 in concrete + 30 grout + 12 plate + washer +
                     nut + 5); order 420 and confirm nut/washer sizes.
  Installation ..... cast in, hand tightened (not torqued).
  Head ............. one square plate per rod, 50 x 50 x 12 A36 (10 also works),
                     centre hole 18, washer + nut behind. The two plates of a
                     row may be tied together for placement (PENDIENTE 5).

BASTONES (anchor reinforcement for the tension row, all rod tension)
  Bars ............. 2 phi12, fy 4200 kg/cm2
  Position ......... x = -82 and +82, y = 80 (beside the VCM 2nd-row corner pair,
                     under the VCS top bars); 56.6 from the tension rods.
  Hook ............. standard 90 deg hook, tail horizontal, outer face of the
                     hook at z = 66 (against the inner face of the column face
                     bars). ldh needed 150, available 205.
  Straight leg ..... along z, toward the VCM: length >= 1217 from the hook outer
                     face (ld = 1011 "other cases"), i.e. the bar ends >= 883
                     past the column back face, inside the VCM (PENDIENTE 4).

JOINT (y = 0 to 350)
  - Hoop layers at y = 120 and y = 240 (>= 2 layers, spacing <= 200). Keep them
    away from y = 42, 80 and 198 (rods and bastones) by >= 13.
  - Column phi16 bars end in standard hooks turned toward the column centre.
  - Steel-column anchors (4 phi12 at x = +/-100, z = 100 and 300) cross the
    joint: 6 clear in plan to the bastones (PENDIENTE 7).
