# archived

Superseded designs, kept for reference. Not maintained: paths to other folders
(printers, catalogs) may be broken, and nothing here is used by lib/ or casa_saav/.
PDFs and figures no report uses were removed on 2026-10-09 (still in git history).

- `cantilever_anchor/rev2_issued`: IPE 200 cantilever on a concrete pedestal,
  revision 2 (its shop drawings were issued).
- `cantilever_anchor/V2`: revision 3 variant, IPE 240, two platinas.
- `cantilever_anchor/double_plate`: through-rods and a bolted end plate (scheme).
- `cantilever_anchor/bolted_plate`: check of the RAM Connection bolted-plate design.

Their HTML reports read as study guides (anchorage, STM, yield lines); some
may move to learning/.
