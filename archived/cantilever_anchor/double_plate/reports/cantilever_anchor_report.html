3. Open items and notes

  • Concrete beams behind the column (frame item, same for any connection). The cantilever moment at the far face of the column is 32.9 kN m (edge) and 25.5 kN m (corner). Against the room left in the beams quoted earlier (32 and 16 kN m, VCM and VCS), that is 1.03 and 1.59. Part of the moment goes into the pedestal and the steel column instead, which this check ignores. The support moments from the frame model (with the cantilevers) are needed to close this. If they confirm the shortfall at the corner, add top bars to the VCS over the support.
  • Beam lateral-torsional buckling, deflection and the beam itself: same IPE 240, loads and length as V2, checked there (LTB with 2.5 L).
  • No concrete shear breakout: the shear acts downward and the column continues below (the cold joint is not an edge). Tension rods take only the torsion shear (interaction not needed, 17.8.1).
  • End plate: two models agree (yield lines and a lower-bound strip); no prying at the demand. Connection treated as rigid: a 20 mm plate on B7 rods tightened snug + 1/3 turn with the compression flange bearing steel on steel.
  • Hold points: corner hooks 7 mm over the rods of X; top ties placed before the rods; threads protected; front plate vented and vibrated.
  • Nuts: tension rods snug tight plus 1/3 turn; shear rods snug tight.
