Border beam at the slab edge: IPE 160, A36, simply supported between cantilever tips

2026-10-03, design proposal (not in the drawings). Catalog: IPAC Nacional 2023 (every IPE and every rectangular tube with H ≥ 100). AISC 360-16 LRFD; vibration per AISC Design Guide 11, 2nd ed.

1. Layout and loads (assumptions to confirm)

  1. Where. Along the slab edge, between the tips of the IPE 240 cantilevers: south edge B9–C9–D9 (spans 4780), east edge E4–E3 (span 4330). Each span pinned at both ends (shear tab on the cantilever tip).
  2. Deck. Spans from the grid-4 beam (south strip) or the grid-D beam (east strip) to the border beam, 1270 mm. Simple span: the border beam takes half, 635 mm of floor.
  3. Loads. qD = 2.5, qL = 2 kN/m² (as the cantilevers) + self-weight + railing 0.3 kN/m (assumed: steel tube railing with posts, about 30 kg/m; no data). Its posts are anchored into the slab concrete, so the horizontal push on the railing goes into the slab, not into the beam. 1.2D + Ev + L with Ev = 0.579 D (NEC, as the cantilevers), 1.2D + 1.6L, 1.4D.
  4. Corner E9. Two pinned pieces cannot meet at E9 (no column there). Proposal: the east border beam runs in one piece E3–E4–E9 (4330 + 1270 = 5600, one 6 m bar) and cantilevers 1270 past the tip of CX; the short south piece D9–E9 is pinned to the tip of CY and to the end of that cantilever. Its moment there is small (section 5).
  5. Top flange braced by the deck (deck screwed or welded to it at every rib before the pour). The deck rests on one side of the beam; once it is fixed, it also holds the beam against twisting.

3. IPE 160: strength and deflection

  1. wD = 2.5 × 0.635 + 0.15 (self-weight) = 2.04 kN/m; wL = 2 × 0.635 = 1.27 kN/m; wu = max(1.4D, 1.2D + 1.6L, 1.779D + L) = 4.90 kN/m.
  2. Mu = wu L²/8 = 4.90 × 4.78²/8 = 14.0 kN m; compact, top flange braced: φMn = 0.9 Fy Zx = 0.9 × 248 × 124.0 cm³ = 27.7 kN m; D/C 0.51 (F2.1).
  3. Vu = 11.7 kN; φVn = 119.0 kN; D/C 0.10 (G2.1).
  4. Deflection, bare steel (I = 869 cm⁴): live 5.0 mm (L/360 = 13.3), dead + live 13.0 mm (L/240 = 19.9); D/C 0.37 / 0.65.
  5. During the pour (deck fixed, wet concrete 2.0 kN/m² + 1.0 construction load) the moment is below the one above.

4. IPE 160: vibration (DG11)

  1. Weight for vibration (actual, not design; DG11 3.3): qD + 0.29 kPa (residence, Table 3-1): w = 1.93 kN/m on the beam.
  2. Composite stiffness (DG11 3.2: the deck fixed to the beam is enough): concrete above the deck 55 mm, width min(635, 0.2L) = 635 mm, Ec = 4700√f'c = 21329 MPa, dynamic n = Es/(1.35 Ec) = 6.95: It = 4788 cm⁴ (bare 869).
  3. Beam: Δj = 5wL⁴/(384 E It) = 1.58 mm, fj = 0.18√(g/Δ) = 14.2 Hz.
  4. Supports: each cantilever tip carries a whole span of the strip (10.6 kN). Tip flexibility 0.170 mm/kN from the IPE 240 (0.052) and the stretch of the 2 A1 rods over their lever arm (0.118): Δg = 1.81 mm.
  5. Edge strip (DG11 eq. 3-4): fn = 0.18√(g/(Δj + Δg)) = 9.7 Hz ≥ 9 Hz: walking cannot drive it into resonance (DG11 2.2.1). With the connection twice as flexible: 8.3 Hz.
  6. Point stiffness: 1 kN at midspan moves it 0.32 mm (beam + half the tip movement), against 1 mm, the usual limit for residential floors above 8 Hz (EN 1995-1-1 7.3.3 uses 1 to 1.5 mm).
  7. DG11 acceleration (eq. 2-10, 9 to 15 Hz): free edge, Cj = 1, panel width limited to 2/3 of the 1270 strip = 847 mm, W = 14.2 kN; β = 0.02: a/g = 14% against 0.5%. Every section in the table gives 5 to 19%, IPE 240 included: the border beam does not control it. A footstep moves a strip that weighs about 1.2 t; DG11's limit is calibrated on office bays of 100 to 400 kN, and its two equations (below and above 9 Hz) do not even agree for a panel this light. What changes it is mass and damping at the edge (a wall or cladding fixed to the edge, partitions), not a deeper beam. For a light floor of this size the realistic targets are the two above.

5. Effect on the cantilevers (the anchorage)

Your ETABS model already has the border beam and the deck spanning to it, so the design moments stay; what is new is the railing, which reaches each cantilever tip as a point load: ΔMu = 1.779 × 0.3 kN/m × (beam length on the tip) × 1.12 m. Beam length on each tip: E a whole span (4780), C1 half a span + 150, CX and CY half a span + the 1270 corner piece (envelope).

6b. Connection to the cantilever tips

  1. Detail (user 2026-10-04): a tip plate PL 10x150x240 A36 welded across the end of each IPE 240 (fillets 5 all around). The IPE 160 web is bolted flat to it; its inner half-flanges, top and bottom, are cut flush with the web over the plate plus 10 each side (they would hit the cantilever). Bolts M16 A325 (or 8.8) in holes Ø18, columns at 35 on each side of the cantilever web, rows at z = -50 and -110. Where the beam passes a tip (B9, E3, E4) it takes all 4 bolts, centred on the web; where two beams end at a tip (C9, D9) each takes the 2 on its side, 10 mm apart at the ends.
  2. Reaction per beam end (1.2D + Ev + L, railing included): Ru = 11.7 kN.
  3. Bolts (J3.6): φRn = 0.75 × 372 × 201 = 56.1 kN per bolt; 2 bolts: 112.2 kN against 11.7: D/C 0.10.
  4. Bearing on the web of the IPE 160, t = 5 (J3.10(a)): 0.75 × 2.4 × 16 × 5 × 400 = 57.6 kN per bolt; 2 bolts: D/C 0.10.
  5. Coped end: shear on the whole web (G2.1) 1.0 × 0.6 × 248 × 160 × 5 = 119 kN: D/C 0.10. Bending where the cut ends, 50 from the bolts: M = 0.59 kN m against 0.9 Fy Z (Z without the inner half-flanges = 81 cm³) = 18.0 kN m: D/C 0.03.
  6. Tip plate welds (J2.4, E70): web fillets 5 both sides, 190.4 long: φRn = 0.75 × 0.6 × 483 × 0.707 × 5 × 2 × 190.4 = 293 kN against 2 Ru = 23.4 kN: D/C 0.08 (the flange fillets are extra).
  7. Torsion on the cantilevers (what the anchorage calc assumes: 0.25 Vu b/2, i.e. Vu at 15 mm): B9, E3, E4: bolt group centred on the web, none. C9 (E): live load on one span only, 3.0 kN at 35 mm = 0.11 kN m against 0.59 assumed. D9 (CY): span C–D 11.7 kN and corner piece 3.1 kN on either side, (11.7 − 3.1) × 35 = 0.30 kN m against 0.46 assumed. Both within.
  8. Corner E9: the corner piece D9–E9 frames into the web of the east beam: shear tab PL 8x90x120 welded to that web, 2 bolts; the piece's end coped top and bottom (17 deep, 51 long). Reaction 3.1 kN: a fraction of the checks above.
