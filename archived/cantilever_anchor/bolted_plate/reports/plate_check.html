End plate check: PL 12x160x350 A36 + extra plate PL 12 A36, rib PL 12

2026-10-02, review page. Type E (edge) is written out step by step; the table at the end gives the same steps for the four types. Every length in the steps is drawn with a dimension line in the diagram next to it. Units: mm, kN, MPa; plate moments in kN m per m of width (= N mm/mm ÷ 1000).

Method. Strips, a lower bound: each strip is a cantilever carrying a share of an anchor force to a welded support, and its width counts only where it lands on that support. If every strip stays below φmp, the plate is safe whatever the real distribution. Yield lines are not used.

1. Forces on the plate

  1. Design moment at the column face, from your ETABS cases: Mu = (1.2 + Ev) MD + ML = (1.2 + 0.579)(8.85) + 6.34 = 22.08 kN m.
  2. Bearing stress, AISC DG1 3.1.1 with √(A2/A1) = 1: fp = 0.65 × 0.85 × f'c = 0.65 × 0.85 × 20.59 = 11.38 MPa; per mm of height q = fp B = 11.38 × 160 = 1821 N/mm.
  3. Anchors to the bottom edge: f + N/2 = pf + d + 25 = 29 + 240 + 25 = 294 mm.
  4. Bearing length (DG1 Eq. 3.4.3, P = 0): Y = (f + N/2) − √[(f + N/2)² − 2 Mu/q] = 294 − √[294² − 2 × 22.08×106/1821] = 44.6 mm.
  5. Pull on the two top anchors: T = q Y = 1821 × 44.6 = 81.3 kN; per anchor Ta = 40.6 kN. Lever arm = 294 − Y/2 = 271.7 mm.
  6. Plate capacity per unit width: φmp = 0.9 Fy t²/4 = 0.9 × 248 × 12²/4 = 8.04 kN m/m (end plate and extra plate alike).

The bearing block at full fp at the bottom edge assumes a stiff plate. Check 4 shows the plate can deliver it.

2. Tension side: the plate around each top anchor

The end plate is held in two places only, both on the beam side: the fillet of the top flange (support 1) and the fillet of the rib (support 2). A strip can only end on one of them. Lengths are measured to the toe of each fillet:

  • support 1 at z = w = 8, over |v| ≤ bf/2 + w = 68;
  • support 2 at |v| = trib/2 + wrib = 6 + 6 = 12, from z = 8 to the plate top 85.

Anchor: vT = 35, pf = 29.

End plate (plate 1)

  1. Strip 1, to the flange: span x1 = pf − w = 29 − 8 = 21; width 2x1 = 42 centred on the anchor (v = 14 to 56), all on support 1 (12 to 68): b1 = 42.
  2. Strip 2, to the rib: span x2 = vT − 12 = 35 − 12 = 23; width 2x2 = 46 (z = 6 to 52), cut to the rib between z = 8 and 85: b2 = 44.
  3. A force F in a strip gives at its support m = F x/b per unit width: strip 1, x1/b1 = 0.5000; strip 2, x2/b2 = 0.5227 (per kN, in kN m/m).
  4. Share α to strip 1 so both strips have the same moment: α = (x2/b2)/(x1/b1 + x2/b2) = 0.511; then m = 0.2556 per kN carried by plate 1.

Extra plate (plate 2), 12 mm, two pieces with a 24 mm gap over the rib, fillets 6 on the bottom and inner edges only (the outer and top edges are flush with the end plate)

  1. It gets its share by contact: the nut pushes plate 1 onto it around the anchor (compression, so no weld is needed there).
  2. Its supports are its own edge welds, which sit right over support 1 (bottom edge, z = 0) and support 2 (inner edge, over the rib).
  3. Strip A, to the bottom edge: y1 = pf − 0 = 29; width 2y1 = 58 (v = 6 to 64), cut to the piece (12 to 80): 52.
  4. Strip B, to the inner edge: y2 = vT − gap/2 = 35 − 12 = 23; width 2y2 = 46 (z = 6 to 52), cut to the piece (0 to 85): 46.
  5. Same split: m = 0.2636 per kN carried by plate 2.

Result, type E

  1. Share between the plates so that both reach the same ratio: plate 1 takes β = 0.51 of Ta, plate 2 the rest (20.0 kN).
  2. Moment in each plate: m = β × 0.2556 × Ta = 0.51 × 0.2556 × 40.6 = 5.27 kN m/m.
  3. D/C = 5.27 / 8.04 = 0.66
  4. Extra strict, plate stays elastic (DG16 thick-plate idea applied to the strips): 1.11 m / φmp = 0.73
  5. Where strip 1 and strip 2 cross (near the corner of the two supports) both moments are equal and of the same sign; that is within the yield criterion of the plate (square or von Mises).
  6. Without the extra plate (β = 1): 0.26 × 40.6 = 10.39 kN m/m, D/C 1.29; it is needed.

3. Prying

  1. AISC Manual Part 9: b' = b − db/2 = 29 − 7.9375 = 21.0625; p = 72.5.
  2. Thickness with no prying: tmin = √[4 Ta b'/(φ p Fu)] = √[4 × 40643 × 21.0625/(0.9 × 72.5 × 400)] = 11.5 mm.
  3. Two plates, not composite: teff = √(12² + 12²) = 17.0 mm (same plastic capacity).
  4. D/C = 11.5 / 17.0 = 0.67. The strict check of step 2 gives 0.73.

4. Bearing side, below the bottom flange

  1. Strip below the flange (DG1 3.1.2): m = 25 + 0.025 d = 25 + 6 = 31 (from the plate bottom to the 0.95 d line).
  2. Strips beyond the flange tips: n = (B − 0.8 bf)/2 = (160 − 96)/2 = 32.
  3. Y = 44.6 ≥ m and ≥ n: the whole strip is loaded at fp (DG1 Eq. 3.3.14 form): M = fp × length²/2.
  4. m strip: 11.38 × 31²/2 = 5.47 kN m/m; D/C 0.68.
  5. n strips: 11.38 × 32²/2 = 5.83 kN m/m; D/C 0.73 (governs).
  6. Strict: DG1 itself would use the full allowed pressure on this face, fp = 0.65 × 0.85 f'c √(A2/A1) = 12.35 MPa (√(A2/A1) = 1.09). Then Y = 40.8 and the strips: m 5.94 kN m/m, n 6.32 kN m/m; D/C 0.74 / 0.79.
  7. Only plate 1 here: the extra plate is at the top.
